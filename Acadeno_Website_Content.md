## CONTENT NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- **Tone throughout:** Precise, confident, enterprise-grade. No superlatives, no filler phrases like "cutting-edge" or "world-class" in running body copy — results and specifics do the talking.
- **Headlines:** Short, declarative, active. Should stand alone without body copy.
- **Body copy:** Written at a senior decision-maker reading level — CTO, COO, Head of Engineering. Assume they've seen a hundred vendor websites. Every sentence needs to earn its place.
- **Metrics:** Always surface real numbers — 18 team members, 7+ clients, 16+ service areas — these build credibility far more than generic claims.
- **CTA language:** "Start a Conversation" is intentional — less transactional than "Get a Quote", more committed than "Learn More". Maintains the consultative, peer-level tone.
- **Process section:** Strongly recommended addition — enterprise buyers want to understand exactly how an engagement works before they reach out. Reduces friction significantly.
