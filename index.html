Who We Are

We don't rebuild what works. We make it work smarter.

Acadeno Technologies Private Limited is an enterprise software development and AI engineering company headquartered in Calicut, Kerala — operating from Govt Cyberpark, one of India's most advanced SEZ technology hubs.

Founded in 2025, we were built around a single, focused belief: that artificial intelligence should integrate into the tools your business already depends on — not replace them. Our team of AI specialists, full-stack engineers, and systems architects works across industries and geographies to solve one of the most persistent challenges in enterprise technology today: making legacy and existing software intelligent, without the cost and complexity of rebuilding.

We serve clients across the USA, UK, Europe, and the Middle East — delivering solutions that are precise in execution, transparent in pricing, and built to perform at scale.

Our Focus

AI integration into software that already exists.

Most businesses aren't lacking software. They're lacking intelligence inside the software they already have. At Acadeno, our primary specialisation is implementing AI modules — machine learning, NLP, predictive analytics, and automation — directly into platforms your teams already use. Whether the system was built in-house or by a third-party vendor, we work with you to layer intelligence into it without disrupting existing workflows.

Beyond AI integration, we deliver a complete suite of technology services: from full-stack web and mobile development to IoT, embedded systems, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity — giving enterprise clients a single, accountable engineering partner across their entire technology landscape.

How We Work

From first conversation to live deployment.
